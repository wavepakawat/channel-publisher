Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 October 2026

Channel Publisher is a private command-line tool used only by its developer on the developer's own channels. This policy applies to anyone who authorizes the tool with a Google account.

YouTube API Services

Channel Publisher uses YouTube API Services. By using the tool you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Information accessed

  • The ID of the YouTube channel you authorize, to confirm that a video goes to the intended channel.
  • The privacy and processing status of videos uploaded through the tool.

The tool sends video files and their metadata to YouTube. It does not read analytics, subscriber data, viewer data, or other people's channels.

Information stored

  • The OAuth token for each authorized channel, stored in a file on the developer's computer that only that user account can read.
  • A log of uploads with the video ID, link, privacy status and time, stored on the same computer. Entries are deleted the next time the tool runs once they are 30 days old.

Channel Publisher has no server. Nothing is stored anywhere except that computer and YouTube.

Sharing

Information is sent only to Google and YouTube, as needed to upload videos. It is not shared with any other internal or external party, and it is not sold.

Cookies and advertising

The tool does not place cookies or similar technology on your device and does not allow third parties to serve content or advertisements. This website is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages and sets no cookies of its own.

Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke the tool's access at any time on the Google security settings page. The revoke command of the tool also revokes the token and immediately deletes the stored token and upload log. If access is revoked from the Google page, stored data is deleted within 30 days. Requests to delete data are completed within 7 days.
